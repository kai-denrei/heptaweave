Ring Stave Numerals

The ring is the stave. Each digit stands directly on it, and the Cistercian digit logic is unchanged: along the ring is “up the stave”, out of the ring is “out”.

In a Cistercian numeral the digits hang off one vertical stave, and every digit shape is a small figure attached to it at one or two points. Here the stave bends into the heptaweave’s ring. A digit is drawn in the ring’s own frame at its slot: along the ring (tangential) is what “up the stave” used to be, out of the ring (radial) is what “out from the stave” used to be. No stems: the figures stand on the ring itself.

Nothing about the digit shapes changes. 1 and 2 are radial bars at the tip and foot points, 3 and 4 are diagonals, 5 closes into a triangle with the ring, 6 stands apart, parallel to the ring, 7 and 8 are bowls, 9 is a box on the ring.

The ten shapes, on the ring

Ring frame: a digit spans two points on the ring, its tip T and its foot F, an arc of 0.5 R apart, and reaches out by w = 0.65 of that. Zero draws nothing: an empty slot is a bare stretch of ring.

Eight slots, from the thin end to the thick end

How it is written in ink

Same growth front as the Cistercian prompt. The front starts at the thin end and runs the one way round to the thick end, the brush swelling as it goes; as it passes a slot it runs out into that digit’s figure from the figure’s foot on the ring, so the units appear first and the highest place last. Digit 6 keeps its own seed, a beat after the front passes its slot. Built: src/cistercian/ringStave.js, drawn wherever the growth painter draws when the lab’s ring-stave switch is on; the slot count is the lab’s ring-stave slots.

Settled

  • No stems. The ring is the stave; figures stand on it. (Operator, 2026-09-10.)
  • Slots. Eight by default, room for sixteen, sharing the arc. Reading starts at the thin end (bottom right, units) and ends at the thick end (bottom left, highest place). A number uses only as many slots as it has digits; the left counter runs to 108 − 1.
  • Outside. Figures reach outward; the ring is sized so the widest figure still fits the cell.
  • Mirroring. Adjacent slots swap tip and foot; nothing else.
  • Opening. At the bottom, so both ends are in view: thin at the right, thick at the left. The growth front starts at the thin end.
